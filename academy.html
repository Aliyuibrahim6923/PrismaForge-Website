FAQ

Everything you need to know before you apply.

Can't find an answer? Reach out to our team — we usually reply within one business day.

Do I need prior experience to enroll?

Not for our foundational courses — they're designed for complete beginners. Advanced tracks (e.g. AI/ML, Cloud DevOps) recommend prior programming basics, which our intro courses cover.

Are courses online, in-person or hybrid?

Most cohorts run online (live + on-demand), with select hybrid options that include in-person labs and bootcamp weekends. Corporate cohorts can be delivered fully onsite.

How are certificates verified?

Each certificate has a unique verification code and shareable link. Employers can confirm authenticity directly on our verification portal.

Do you offer installment payments?

Yes — cohort programs can be paid in 2 or 3 installments. We also offer scholarships and sponsorships for outstanding applicants.

Will you help me get hired?

Yes. Cohort and Graduate Employability Readiness students receive CV/LinkedIn reviews, mock interviews and direct introductions to our hiring partner network.

Can my company train its staff with PrismaForge?

Absolutely. We design custom corporate training programs with skills audits, tailored curriculum, dedicated cohorts and outcome reporting. Talk to our team.
